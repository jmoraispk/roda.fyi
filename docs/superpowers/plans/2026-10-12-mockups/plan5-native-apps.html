Plan 5 · Phone apps

The same Roda app, installed as an iPhone and Android app. Part A builds the apps so you can put them on your own phone now: the app opens with a splash, signs you in through the phone's browser, and works like the web version, with the phone's own status bar, Back button, and home-screen icon. Part B, for later, adds what the App Store and Google Play require before publishing.

Opening the app and signing in

Sign-in happens in the phone's browser, because Google does not allow signing in inside an app's built-in web view. You come back to the app already signed in.
