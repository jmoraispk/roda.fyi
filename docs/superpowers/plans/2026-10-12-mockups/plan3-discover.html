Plan 3 · Discover

Discover v1: people shown per city and discoverable groups on an OpenStreetMap map, search, person and group cards, Discover settings, the owner's group place, and welcome step 3. Only city-level places are stored. Train-mode people stay hidden until they choose otherwise. The map is drawn with shapes here; the app shows real OpenStreetMap tiles. Sample viewer: João Morais (Both, Lisbon).
