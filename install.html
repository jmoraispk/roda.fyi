Put roda on your phone

roda is a web app: there is nothing to download from a store. Add it to your home screen once, and it opens full-screen with its own icon, like any other app.

Open roda

iPhone and iPad

Open roda.fyi/app in Safari and sign in. Then:

  1. Tap ⋯ at the bottom right, next to the address bar. (On older iPhones, tap the Share button, the square with an arrow, in the toolbar and skip to step 3.)
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. If you don't see it, tap Edit Actions at the bottom of the list and add it.
  4. Keep Open as Web App turned on (it appears on iOS 26 and later).
  5. Tap Add. The roda icon appears on your home screen.

Prefer pictures? These guides show each step with screenshots: iPhone Life and iThinkDiff (iOS 26).

Notifications from roda, when they arrive, only work once roda is on your home screen (iOS 16.4 or later).

Android

Open roda.fyi/app in Chrome and sign in. Then:

  1. Tap Install if roda offers it on the Today screen, or tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  2. Tap Install app (on some phones it says Add to Home screen).
  3. Confirm with Install.

Computer

In Chrome or Edge, open roda.fyi/app and click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or choose Install roda from the browser menu. On a Mac with Safari, choose File → Add to Dock.

Good to know

  • It's free, and the same app works on every phone and computer.
  • Updates arrive by themselves: there's nothing to update.
  • Your trainings are saved to your account, so you can sign in on another device and find everything there.
